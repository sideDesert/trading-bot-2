## Findings

- [S1][S2][S4] Retail F&O base rates (SEBI studies, escalating versions): FY22–FY24 → 93% of individuals lost (₹1.81 lakh cr, avg ₹2 lakh/trader); FY25 → ~91% lost, ₹1.06 lakh cr net loss (+41% YoY); FY26 → 87.7% lost, ₹91,685 cr net loss, avg loss/trader *rose* to ₹1.17 lakh. The share of losers is basically invariant; regulation shrank participation (active traders −20% in FY26, new entrants −40%), not loss proportion.
- [S1][S6] ~92% of individual losses come from **options**; 97% of individual traders are primarily/exclusively **option buyers**. Options *sellers* were the only strategy group with positive median return on capital employed in FY26 [S1]. Buying-side scalping fights the single highest-mortality strategy in the market.
- [S1] Loss rates scale with wealth: 93% loss rate for traders with no equity holdings → 58% for >₹10 cr portfolios; small-portfolio (<₹1 lakh) traders = ~70% of aggregate losses on ~half the turnover.
- [S2][S6] The counterparty reality: in FY24, 96–97% of FPI and proprietary-firm profits came from **algo entities**; FY26 gross books: prop +₹44,000 cr, FPI +₹14,000 cr, corporates +₹8,000 cr vs individuals −₹72,000 cr gross plus ~₹25,000 cr transaction costs [S6]. A retail scalping bot competes directly against institutional algos for the same door.
- [S7][S8][S12] Entry-cost floor (since Nov 20, 2024): min contract value ₹15 lakh → NIFTY lot 25→75. One NIFTY lot notional ≈ ₹15–18 lakh (round-trip point risk: 75 units × NIFTY point moves). A 100-pt adverse move on 1 lot ≈ ₹7,500.
- [S9] Post Nov 20, 2024: **only one weekly index per exchange** — NSE has NIFTY weekly only; BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY weeklies are dead (monthly/quarterly remain). BSE has SENSEX weekly only. Contract choice for weekly scalping: NIFTY or SENSEX, nothing else.
- [S10] Since **Sept 1, 2025 all NSE expiries are on Tuesday** (first NIFTY Tuesday weekly expiry: Sept 2, 2025); BSE/SENSEX expiries are Thursday. Weekly expiry-day calendars can therefore be "twin-expiry weeks" (Tue NIFTY + Thu SENSEX).
- [S11][S13] From **Oct 1, 2025** intraday index-options limits per entity (per index, FutEq delta basis): NET ₹5,000 cr, GROSS ₹10,000 cr each side (EOD: ₹1,500 cr net). ≥4 random snapshots/day, one mandatory 14:45–15:30. Expiry-day breaches attract ASD/penalty from Dec 6, 2025. Irrelevant to small retail size, but it structurally limits big directional players on expiry day and changed late-day expiry dynamics.
- [S7] Effective Feb 1, 2025: **upfront premium collection** for option buys (no intraday leverage beyond collateral — premium is debited immediately) and **no calendar-spread margin benefit on expiry day** for the expiring leg; plus **+2% extreme loss margin on short options on expiry day** since Nov 20, 2024 [S24: short-side expiry margin ≈ ₹1.65–1.7 lakh/lot vs ~₹1.5L normal].
- [S14][S15][S17] Upstox API daily workflow: OAuth 2.0 + 2FA every day; token dies **3:30 AM IST next day** regardless of issue time; no long-lived order token. OpenAlgo's 32-broker audit confirms the classic bug: WS adapters reconnect with a stale token after the overnight rollover → feed silently dead next morning ("works after restart, dead next morning"). Bot must re-auth pre-open daily and re-read a fresh token on every reconnect.
- [S16][S17][S20] Upstox reliability: recurring WS drops (close code 1000, minute-scale), silent data stalls → need stall watchdog + reconnect + resubscribe (OpenAlgo #1357); user-documented incidents include peak-hour chart freezes, 2–3 s option-quote lag, stale-feed incident (Oct 26, 2023), portfolio mix-up (Mar 19, 2025), Cloudflare knock-on outages. Plan for feed staleness detection and order-path redundancy; do not treat Upstox WS as trustworthy at expiry-day close.
- [S18][S19] Rate limits: Upstox enforces per-API/per-user limits with **no remaining-quota headers** — first signal of breach is HTTP 429 (UDAPI10005). Full option-chain REST endpoints are deliberately throttled (community: 1 call/3 s on full-chain endpoints across brokers) → polling chains is impractical; use market-quote API for specific strikes or WS subscriptions. Order API shares a combined limit; ≥10 orders/sec crosses into SEBI/NEST "registered algo" territory — keep <10 orders/s to stay a regular retail algo (no exchange registration).
- [S21][S22][S23] Expiry-day (now Tuesday) microstructure: opening range wider, mid-session "dead zone" 12:30–14:00, then gamma resolution into 15:30. India VIX typically **falls 3–5% on expiry day** (elevated morning → sag midday → can re-spike final hour); a *rising* VIX after 1 PM is a warning sign. Theta: ~75% of ATM premium gone by ~2 PM; OTM strikes lose ~89%+ on range days. Expiry-day buyers must be right **and** early; late entries decay even when directionally correct [S23]. For a scalper this favors (a) early-window momentum trades, (b) flat by 14:45, (c) extra caution when VIX > 16 (halve size [S22]).
- [S25][S26][S27][S28] What publicly documented winners actually do: (i) tiny deployed capital vs net worth — Thakre deployed ~₹8 lakh/day on ₹1 cr capital; (ii) majority are **sellers/theta-side or momentum scalpers buying only in confirmed direction**, never OTM lottery tickets; (iii) hard daily kill-switch ("close laptop at ±₹70k" — Karthik Raj protocol; Jitendra Jain "trade with max loss in mind"); (iv) instrument focus (Nifty only), execution discipline > new indicators; (v) full-time attention — documented sellers (Dhiraj Sinha, CapMint) warn part-time option trading fails. None of the verified cases is a passive scheduled-alert bot; all combine system + discretionary risk gating.

## Implications for our bot

- **Capital assumptions:** Realistic floor ≈ ₹10–15 lakh trading capital. Rationale: 1-lot short positions need ~₹1.5–1.7L margin (₹1.7L on expiry) → to run 2–4 concurrent positions with 30–40% free buffer you need ₹8–12L; premium-buying scalping needs ≥₹3–5L to survive the variance of 75-unit lots. Do not market to sub-₹3 lakh users; SEBI data shows sub-₹1 lakh portfolio traders are the 93%-loser cohort bankrolling the market.
- **Realistic expectancy:** Base rate is 88–93% losers; the only positive-median cohort is option *sellers* [S1]. Design around theta-side or quick momentum scalps, not OTM premium buying. Verified scalper case: ₹2.16 cr on ₹1 cr with ~₹8L deployed ⇒ ~aggressive return on deployed, ~low-double-digit % on total capital, achieved by elite execution. Advisory bot should promise risk-controlled process, not returns; expectancy of an average user following signals remains negative after ~₹25,000 cr/yr of friction (costs).
- **Contracts/days:** Trade NIFTY weekly (Tuesday expiry) — the deepest liquidity. Avoid: (a) OTM option buys after ~12:30 on expiry day (89% decay base rate [S23]); (b) holding anything into 14:45–15:30 expiry window unless deliberately trading the gamma resolution (also the surveillance-snapshot window); (c) BANKNIFTY mid-month illiquid strikes; (d) Mondays on expiry week as blind directional scalps (weekend gap + Tuesday theta regime).
- **Ops requirements (hard):** daily 6:30–8:30 AM IST token refresh job; fresh-token-on-reconnect WS architecture; data-stall watchdog (90 s); staleness kill-switch (if quote age > 2–3 s on expiry day, halt signals — Upstox lag incidents); backoff on 429s; stay <10 orders/s; option-chain via WS subscriptions or strike-level quotes, never full-chain polling.
- **Risk gating is the product:** Every verified winner (Thakre, Karthik Raj, Jain, Jegan) is differentiated by loss discipline (kill-switch, max-loss exit, tiny deployed fraction) — not prediction quality. The bot's core features should be: per-day loss lockout, per-trade max-loss brackets enforced broker-side, size-halving when India VIX > 16, and expiry-day afternoon flattening.

- Upstox's exact current per-endpoint rate-limit numbers (docs page moved; pages 404)— must be scraped from live docs/docs PDF before build; community says option-chain is the throttled outlier.

- Has the Oct-2025 intraday FutEq limit measurably damped expiry-afternoon ranges? Needs post-Dec-2025 quantitative check (ASD penalty regime).
- SEBI Aug-2026 FY26 study shows buyer-only cohort median returns — does any slice of *systematic* retail buyers (event-days only, early-window only) show positive expectancy? Study doesn't decompose by time-in-force; we must backtest ourselves.
