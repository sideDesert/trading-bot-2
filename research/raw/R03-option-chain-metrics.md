# R03: Option-chain metrics' predictive power for near-term index moves (evidence vs folklore)

Date compiled: 2026-09-16. Scope: (a) PCR variants, (b) OI-change interpretation, (c) max pain/pinning, (d) informed option volume, (e) OI-at-strike as S/R, (f) NSE/India-specific studies. Bottom line: the peer-reviewed edge lives in *signed/volume-based* options data and *cross-sectional* stock-level signals; the retail-favorite *OI-level* signals (PCR from static OI, "highest Call OI = resistance", max pain) have weak-to-no standalone evidence, especially for indices and at intraday horizons.

## Findings

- [S1][S2][S5] **Options volume genuinely leads stock prices.** Buyer-initiated put-call volume ratio (CBOE signed data, 1990–2001): lowest-quintile PCR stocks beat highest-quintile by **>40bp on the next day and >1% over the next week**, risk-adjusted; effect decays over several weeks [S1]. Similar positive/negative-news option volumes predict moves [S2]. ~17% of overall price discovery occurs in the option market [S5]. Caveat: all are *single-stock*, US studies.
- [S4][S6][S7] **Which option-flow signals carry the edge:** new-position *call purchases* are the strongest predictor (leverage-seeking informed flow) [S4]; call-minus-put implied-volatility gaps predict +50bp/week [S6]; OTM-put/ATM-call IV smirk steepness predicts −10.9%/yr relative return with ≥6-month persistence [S7].
- [S3] **O/S ratio works on public EOD data:** high option-to-stock volume → future underperformance; +0.34%/week hedge return (19.3% annualized), significant only ~1 week out — but this is a *cross-sectional stock* effect, not an index timing effect.
- [S21] **Horizon-dependence:** P/C ratio predictive content is fleeting (daily, partly weekly); O/S persists at weekly–monthly. Intraday predictive lifetimes are shorter still.
- [S8][S9][S10] **Pinning is real (a *mechanical* effect, not manipulation-only):** on expiration dates optionable stocks cluster at strikes; average return distortion ≥16.5bp (~$9bn aggregate market-cap shift per expiry; US data, 1996–2002) [S8]. Mechanism: MM delta-hedging flows around large-OI strikes [S9]; confirmed for the S&P 500 index via futures [S10].
- [S11][S12][S13] **"Max pain" as a price target fails as stated.** The apparent predictability is mostly *reversal of recently crashed stocks* concentrated in small/illiquid names, with possible manipulation (max-pain long-short portfolios earn excess returns, but driver ≠ max-pain strike) [S11]. Live graded test: only 41.7% of 242 expirations closed closer to max pain, and median distance widened 2.6%→3.4% [S12]. India-specific backtest: 47% pin rate = coin flip [S13]. Distinction that matters: *pinning to a high-OI strike* (evidence: yes) vs *max-pain strike specifically attracting price* (evidence: no).
- [S14] vs [S15][S16] **India PCR/OI evidence is contradictory and era-dependent.** Early NSE era (SSRN 695745): OI-based predictors significant for NIFTY spot in both sub-periods; volume-based predictors became significant only in the *later* sub-period [S14]. But a 2022–23 NIFTY study found Call-Put ratio (volume and OI versions) **not** predictive for the index [S15], and a 120-day 2026 NIFTY backtest found no profitable or statistically reliable PCR buckets after costs (best bucket 60% win rate on n=10 — noise) [S16].
- [S17][S18] **Index options prices lead NIFTY cash at short (hourly) horizons:** call options lead cash by up to 1 hour; put market shows feedback both ways [S17]; B-S-implied index from calls leads spot, and the lead is *strongest on expiry days* [S18]. This is price-level lead-lag (implied index vs spot), not OI — i.e., **synthetic future / put-call-parity implied level > OI folklore**.
- [S19][S20][S22] **Intraday mechanics usable by a scalping bot:** last-30-min returns are strongly predicted by the rest-of-day return (market intraday momentum) across 60+ futures, traced to gamma-hedging demand [S19]; market-maker net gamma estimated from chain OI predicts *intraday volatility* (positive gamma → lower vol + stronger intraday reversal; negative gamma → higher vol + momentum) [S20]; daily GEX changes positively associate with next-day S&P returns and improve out-of-sample forecasts [S22]. Gamma regime gives *volatility/mean-reversion regime* signals more than direction.
- [S23] **India VIX (chain-derived IV) is the best realized-vol forecast** and subsumes historical vol info [S23] — compute it (or ATM IV) as the bots expected-move input.
- [S24] **Anti-example:** claims of 99.2% correlation between highest-OI strike and next-day close reveal data leakage/bad methodology — useful as a marker that Indian "OI band" literature is mostly low-quality; no credible academic study validates strike-level S/R from OI.
- Unverifiable folklore: Indian retail sites claim OI-derived S/R "holds ~70–80% of the time under normal conditions" [niftytrader.in / marketnetra/algotest blogs] — **no source is ever cited; no peer-reviewed study exists.**

## Implications for our bot

Compute (evidence-ranked for a NIFTY intraday scalper):

1. **Synthetic-index lead**: implied forward from ATM call–put prices (put-call parity) vs traded futures/spot — the "options lead cash by up to 1h" channel [S17][S18][S6]. Cheap to compute from the chain, directly an Upstox poll.
2. **Gamma regime from chain OI**: dealer net gamma proxy (Σ OI × contract gamma, calls positive/puts negative assuming dealers short), plus distance-to-flip level. Use as a *regime filter* (high gamma → expect mean-reversion & lower vol; low/negative gamma → momentum & vol expansion) [S20][S22][S19]. Also supports avoiding mean-reversion trades in low-gamma regimes.
3. **IV / IV-change & skew**: ATM IV (India VIX proxy) for expected move [S23]; call−put IV gap and OTM-put skew as informed-flow proxies [S6][S7]. Skew steepening intraday = negative-news option demand.
4. **Volume-based PCR / imbalance, not OI-PCR**: signed flow is unavailable on NSE, but volume-implied bought-vs-sold imbalance (trade at ask vs bid per strike) approximates net-buying-pressure [S1][S2]; volume-based predictors gained significance in later NSE studies [S14]. Prefer intraday volume deltas over static OI.
5. **ΔOI × price buildup states**: use only as a confession-of-positioning feature for the LLM (fresh writing at a strike = positioning shift), never as a standalone signal; evidence is mixed and horizon-unproven [S14][S16].
6. **Expiry-day specials**: pinning risk near high-OI strikes in the last hour [S8][S9][S10]; intraday-momentum into close per [S19]; max pain as a *heatmap reference only*, not a target [S11][S12][S13].

Ignore / de-prioritize:
- Static OI-PCR thresholds (0.7/1.3 rules) — no index-level support [S15][S16].
- "Max pain = expiry magnet" — rejected [S12][S13].
- "Highest Call OI = unbreakable resistance" — no credible study; treat high-OI strikes as clustering zones near expiry, not walls [S8] vs folklore.
- Weekly-expiry S/R tables published by Indian brokers/blogs — uncited, unvalidated; the "70–80% of the time" figure has no source.

## Open questions

- None of the signed-flow results ([S1][S2][S4]) are validated on Indian *index* options, where retail-dominated 0DTE-like weekly flow after SEBI's 2024–25 curbs may invert the "informed options trader" composition. Build our own log and backtest volume-imbalance on NIFTY chains.
- Does dealer-gamma sign flip reliably daily on NIFTY? India has no public GEX; our OI×gamma proxy's accuracy vs actual MM books is unknown [S20][S22].
- Pinning magnitude in India post-2019 (weeklies) and post-2025 expiry-day reshuffles (Tue NIFTY expiries) is unstudied; 16.5bp US figure is old (1996–2002).
- Pan-Poteshman effect at *intraday* (not daily) horizon: literature is silent at <1h.
- Do volume-based PCR and IV-spread signals survive a 0.10–0.20% round-trip cost at scalping horizons? All Indian backtests so far say no for OI-PCR [S16]; flow-based variants untested in India.
