## Regulatory record — actions/warnings with dates and specifics

- **2023-12 (disclosed 2025)**: Shanghai police bust "AI智能炒股机器人" ring — 56 suspects, **¥92M+**, unlicensed investment-consulting 非法经营. Software sold at ¥20k–259.8k/yr with promises of "一年3.5倍收益"; preset strategies included automated full-position buys and high-frequency trading that police flagged as engineered to lose client money. (#1)
- **2025-10-11**: Douyin mass-banned illegal stock-recommendation accounts (全仓-call accounts, off-platform funneling); WeChat followed with 禁播/封号. 国家网信办 (cyber regulator) coordinated cleanup of 非法荐股 accounts across Douyin/Kuaishou/Weibo/WeChat — 23,574 items handled per 网信中国. (#1)
- **2025-11-19**: 辽宁证监局 warning letter (警示函) to 中天证券 沈阳枫杨路营业部 — unreported livestreams giving investment advice without reasonable basis, incomplete records. Compliance failure of licensed AI/stream-era advisory. (#1)
- **2026-05-09**: 北京证监局 penalty — AI-rewritten fabricated news about a listed chip company; profit ¥293 combined, **fine ¥450k**. Key precedent: using an AI tool to rewrite/generate false securities info does not reduce liability; defense "AI generate, I didn't verify" rejected. (#3)
- **2026-09 (〔2026〕39号 etc.)**: CSRC penalized 4 individuals for 编造、传播虚假信息, including 卢润凯 **fined ¥200k for AI-generating a false article**; securities practitioner 宗建树 received **5-year market ban**. (#2)
- **Ongoing regional risk warnings (证监局)**: Shenzhen (#9) and Jiangsu (#10) published official typologies of "AI炒股" fraud — tampered backtest data, remote-control "auto-trading" tablets, deepfake (AI换脸) analysts, virtual trading platforms (虚拟盘). Regulators repeatedly state: unlicensed AI-based stock recommendation = 非法荐股, regardless of technology used.

Regulatory through-line: Chinese regulators treat "AI" as neither a defense nor a license exemption. AI-generated content that turns out false → 编造、传播虚假信息 liability; AI-based advice without license → 非法荐股; AI-branded software → 非法经营/诈骗 when returns are faked.

## Documented failure cases — what failed, mechanism, numbers; CN-original terms kept

**A. LLM direct stock-picking/trading (real users, real losses)**
1. **Larry's DeepSeek auto-trading (Sina #6)**: ~−¥30k in one month, >50% drawdown vs hoped "3-month double". Mechanisms: LLM can't read K-line structure (only OCRs labels), misses 情绪行情 (sentiment-driven limit-downs: open-high-then-crash), polled data lag (morning advice built on yesterday's close; intraday updates stale by afternoon).
2. **Luna's DeepSeek advisory (Sina #6)**: +4% over 200 days during a bull run to ~3900 on SSE — underperformed CSI300. Ignored lot rules (最少100股) and fees; recommended ¥200–300/share leaders exceeding her capital; mis-predicted a −6% move (actual −9%), single-day loss ¥1k = 4–5× her norm. LLM's post-mortem was fluent but "始终未能触及问题的核心".
3. **李超's hallucinated context (Sina #6)**: LLM fabricated a memory of user-uploaded PDF holdings ("5月5日，用户上传一版PDF持仓情况…"), persisted after challenge; mixed other users' searched stocks into his portfolio; counted holidays as consecutive decline days; conflated A/H dual listings. His independent judgment: +30% on 20 stocks; AI-assisted returns "大打折扣".
4. **南都 newsroom test (#7)**: AI pick-list follow-up — 9 of 10 recommended stocks down next session; LLM citations lean on self-media of unknown authority (豆包: sources "由公开网络信息整理而得"; DeepSeek: 37 citations incl. 股市 self-media accounts).
5. **HKU live FX benchmark (#15)**: DeepSeek V3.2 and MiniMax 2.5 large losses; no correlation between reasoning-benchmark rank and trading P&L.
6. **Backtest "利润幻影" (#16)**: LLM-agent backtest alpha collapses out-of-knowledge-window — training leakage, not alpha.

**B. AI-branded fraud (the dominant documented loss mode in CN media)**
1. **百域量化/永捷量化 Ponzi (21财经 #5)**: 虚拟盘 — balances are back-office-edited numbers, no real trades; 传销裂变 referral; claimed HK SFC Type-9 parent license (fake); pool ¥4.221B before exit; 300+ known victims; one victim −¥1.6M.
2. **通许县 SSky2050 Ponzi (#4)**: beauty-salon agent "AI机器人炒股…稳赚不赔"; 25% instant bonus hook; staged "system upgrade" withdrawal freezes; 15% "activation fee" squeeze; ~¥1M across 10+ victims; 立案 as 合同诈骗 but 1 year with no progress — enforcement recovery is weak.
3. **Shanghai "AI智能炒股机器人" (police, #1)**: ¥92M across products like "蛟龙出水" strategy bundles; remote 代客操作 as upsell tier.
4. **Regulator-typology patterns (#9, #10)**: 后台篡改回测数据 (backtest falsification), remote-desktop auto-trading tablets, AI换脸 analysts, fake profit screenshots + paid shills (托儿).

## Serious-quant critique synthesis — the strongest arguments from CN practitioners against LLM direct-trading

1. **"AGI不是用来炒股的" — 幻方量化/DeepSeek itself (#11)**. The CN institution with the most GPUs and deepest LLM capability explicitly rejects LLM-as-trader. Their position: alpha comes from proprietary data (10PB+, dozens of sources) and quantitative infrastructure, not from a general model. If the maker of DeepSeek won't trade on DeepSeek, retail "用DeepSeek炒股" is category error.
2. **No autonomous innovation → no black-swan robustness (#11)**: LLMs recombine existing human patterns; they cannot originate strategy, so regime breaks and 黑天鹅 events defeat them (echoed by 氪信科技 CEO 朱明杰).
3. **Hallucination is structural, not patchable — broker QC consensus (#12, #13)**: 华泰联合 calls it 达摩克利斯之剑 requiring 二次校验 + source trace-back; 国金证券 classifies 高频交易/监管报送 as zero-tolerance requiring end-to-end human review. Translation: Chinese brokers who actually run money architect LLMs out of the execution path and into low-tolerance tools only.
4. **逻辑幻觉 & belief/fact confusion (academia, #14)**: GenAI produces fluent but causally baseless financial hypotheses; LLMs can't reliably separate "an actor believes X" from "X is true" — fatal for news-driven signal extraction; and issuers now 算法迎合 (strategically write disclosures to game model sentiment scores), corrupting the input stream itself.
5. **Benchmark smarts ≠ trading P&L (#15)**: HKU live experiment shows zero transfer from reasoning rank to returns; position sizing, risk control, and timing — not cleverness — decide outcomes.
6. **Backtest leakage inflates every demo (#16)**: any LLM showing historical trading performance likely memorized the period; 利润幻影.
7. **Information-source degradation (#7)**: RAG-grounded advice cites promotional self-media; grounding ≠ credibility.

## What's NEW vs our R01/R02/R11 findings

- **[NEW]** CSRC has begun fining people specifically for **AI-generated false market information** (2026 cases, ¥200k–450k fines, 5-year ban) — a liability vector R01/R02 didn't cover: our bot's outputs that turn out false aren't just bad trades, they could be construed as misinformation if shared.
- **[NEW]** DeepSeek/幻方 management's own on-record rejection of LLM trading ("AGI不是用来炒股的") — strongest single-practitioner data point; absent from R01/R02.
- **[NEW]** Regulator-documented **backtest falsification** (后台篡改回测数据) as a standard scam component — validates mandatory independently-recomputable backtests; not in prior files.
- **[NEW]** Broker-internal **hallucination-tolerance tiering** (国金: execution/regulatory = zero-tolerance, human-only) — gives us a concrete Chinese-industry architecture template for where LLMs may/not sit.
- **[CONFIRMS]** R02's accuracy-skepticism: CN media-verified LLM pick performance is poor (9/10 down; −50%/month; underperforming CSI300) — matches, with harder numbers.
- **[CONFIRMS]** R11's retail-protection framing: CN regulators see exactly the same retail-harm pattern India does (unlicensed advice, virtual platforms, guaranteed-return lures).
- **[CONFIRMS]** R01's agent-architecture caveats: context corruption (hallucinated holdings), stale data, and no sentiment/market-regime awareness are confirmed in real CN user losses.
- **[CONTRADICTS]** any residual notion that "a frontier Chinese model has home-market edge": DeepSeek lost money on A-share retail tasks and in HKU's own live FX benchmark — no China-market exception found.

## Translation of risks: which CN-market risks do/don't map to NIFTY India

**Maps directly to NIFTY:**
- **LLM advisory inaccuracy in advisory-bot framing** — identical user-harm path; NIFTY options amplify it (leverage, expiry, theta decay make CN's equity losses look gentle).
- **Hallucination in data-intensive contexts** (wrong holdings, miscounted trading days, conflating instruments) — for us: strike/expiry mixups, calendar/weekly-vs-monthly confusion, mixing NIFTY/BANKNIFTY chains. India has its own holiday/weekly-expiry calendar quirks = same failure surface.
- **Stale-data intraday lag** — LLM advice built on prior close is worthless mid-session; NIFTY weekly-expiry gamma makes this worse, not better.
- **Backtest leakage/幻影利润** — universal; demands strict out-of-window evaluation.
- **Regulatory liability for unlicensed advice & false information** — SEBI's RA regulations + IT Act are analogous to CSRC 非法荐股/虚假信息 enforcement; CN precedent shows regulators actively prosecute AI-generated market misinformation. Assume SEBI will follow.
- **"AI trading" branding attracts scam-adjacent users and touts** — any public marketing must avoid 稳赚不赔-style claims; CN shows these claims are the exact red flags regulators scan for (SEBI ads code equivalent).
- **Zero-tolerance human-review tier for execution** (国金 model) — directly adoptable: LLM never touches order routing; human/deterministic rules own execution, risk limits, and kill-switch.

**Does NOT map (CN-specific):**
- 非法荐股 as an *unlicensed* activity — India has a formal registered Research Analyst path; compliance burden differs (SEBI RA registration + disclosures vs outright prohibition).
- 虚拟盘 fake-APP Ponzis — CN-specific fraud infrastructure (super-app ecosystems, OTC funding via personal transfers); India's exchange-linked apps and UPI traceability reduce, not eliminate, this.
- T+1/no-short retail A-share constraints and 涨跌停 limit-up/down sentiment regimes — CN market microstructure features in the failure cases; NIFTY options are T+0 derivatives — LLM fail modes shift toward levered/option-specific errors instead.
- Quant/HFT crowding + 程序化交易 reporting regimes (交易所撤单率 rules) — CN-specific; SEBI/NSE algo rules differ.
- Deepfake (AI换脸) "analyst" funnels — present in India but less documented; treat as reputational/impersonation risk, lower priority.
