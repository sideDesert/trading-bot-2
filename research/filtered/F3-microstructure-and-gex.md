Vol for BSM greeks: NIFTY-futures-ATM IV if computable from chain, else India-VIX/√252 scaled, else EWMA realized vol — document fallback chain. NIFTY weekly expiry is Tuesday (since 1-Sep-2025).

## GEX verdict

**Build it, but only the reduced local-regime form — not the SqueezeMetrics market-wide product.** The dealer-hedging mechanism is among the most replicated findings in the options literature (Ni–Pearson–Poteshman RFS 2021; Avellaneda–Lipkin 2003; expiry strike-clustering JFE 2005), but every strong test of *public-OI-derived aggregate GEX as a forecaster* fails: rank correlation with next-day realized vol collapses from −0.36 to −0.03 (p=0.18) after VIX/ATM-IV controls (pre-registered 8-year SPY backtest), replication of the original methodology finds r²≈0.15 ex-GFC and zero signed-return relation, and a structural decomposition finds the gamma-feedback channel adds nothing to intraday variance forecasts. On NIFTY the naive call+/put− sign convention is additionally broken — positive ~95% of days — so the market-wide sign is close to a constant and must not be computed. What survives is the *conditioning* form: a per-strike local net dollar-gamma sign computed within ±2% of spot from chain OI and BSM gamma (formula in spec above, deadbanded against flapping), used solely as a multiplicative momentum-vs-reversal selector on the microstructure trigger features, at low weight, only after India VIX and ATM IV are already features. Expected standalone contribution is near zero; its job is to flip trigger interpretation on expiry-heavy tape, nothing more. Pair it with the genuinely corroborated option-side features (OI walls as context, delta×ΔOI hedge flow as 5–15-min regime), which carry the T1 evidence the aggregate-GEX product lacks.

## Demoted/dropped claims + reasons

- **VPIN / order-flow toxicity as predictor** — DROPPED. Andersen–Bondarenko (two T2 papers, superior E-mini tick data): peaked after not before the flash crash, zero incremental power once volume/vol controlled, sign flips under transaction-level signing. BVC classification independently shown least accurate (Chakrabarty et al.). Salvage only its inputs (volume-bucket imbalance, trade intensity).
- **Market-wide signed GEX level/sign** — DROPPED as an input. Naive convention yields +gamma on ~95% of NIFTY days (sign near-constant); providers disagree up to 2,250× on absolute values (only within-platform relative rankings meaningful); FlashAlpha shows redundancy with VIX/ATM IV. Replaced by `gex_local_sign` regime flag.
- **Contemporaneous OFI-as-forecast at ≥1 min** — DROPPED. Cont–Cucuringu–Zhang: strictly-lagged 1-min OOS R² negative-to-nil (−0.4% to −0.1%) despite 87% contemporaneous. OFI kept only as ≤30 s nowcast/trigger.
- **Max pain as magnet/target** — DEMOTED to labelled weak context or skip. Out-of-sample trackers: 41.7% (n=242) beat-snapshot rate with widening median distance; Indian sample ~47%; within-25-pts ~20–25%. Residual signal = generic short-term reversal (SSRN wp).
- **Charm/vanna intraday edge** — DEMOTED to tail/single-source. Statistically significant charm window comes from one practitioner white paper with proprietary dealer data (Volland/DeLorenzo) + industry blogs; no peer-reviewed or NIFTY corroboration. Do not implement greek-flow maps until replicated in-house; delta×ΔOI hedge flow (T1) covers the same channel more robustly. Note for the archive: R05's single strongest decision-relevant suggestion (Tuesday post-noon reversion up-weight) survives via `expiry_regime.last90`, which rests on the corroborated range/concentration stats, not on the charm model.
- **Gamma-flip acceleration trigger / post-OpEx "gamma unclench"** — DEMOTED to display-only context. Desk concepts (Nomura/McElligott via Heisenberg; SpotGamma) with anecdotal validation; the one structural academic test (Princeton dissertation) finds no gamma-channel predictive power.
- **"0DTE/expiry flow destabilizes the index" / crash-avoidance logic** — DROPPED. Weight of evidence (exogenous calendar-variation study: −60–90 bps vol; Cboe re-analyses; ~0.1σ shock response) favors attenuation; single dissenting working paper (+24.5%/σ) outvoted. Normal risk limits suffice.
- **Multi-level integrated (PCA) OFI** — DEMOTED to backlog. 87% in-sample contemporaneous adj-R² is non-tradeable; strictly-lagged fails. Revisit only if trigger ablation shows L1 OFI saturating.
- **Deep-learning universal order-flow model (Sirignano–Cont)** — NOTED, not built. Establishes stationary out-of-sample direction-predictability exists, but no cost-adjusted Sharpe proven; deterministic engine + LLM architecture does not host it. Future-phase candidate.

## Open risks

- **OI latency vs feature freshness**: option-chain REST OI updates on exchange schedule (~minutes-level staleness possible on some strikes despite 1–3 s polling); `opt_hedge_flow` and `gex_local_sign` may reflect stale OI intraday. Mitigation: gate these features on observed OI-change activity; mark stale when no ΔOI for >60 s; do not trade off walls that haven't re-confirmed.
- **Signing accuracy at ~1/s event-batched ticks**: tick rule ≈78.6%/trade (Carrion–Kolay); LR-lite requires quote/tick interleaving that Upstox batching may not preserve. Signed-volume features assume symmetric misclassification — verify empirically on a recorded NIFTY session before arming as trigger; fall back to L1 imbalance (no signing needed) if bias found.
- **GEX sign convention unverifiable**: the local-sign flag inherits the fundamental dealer-inventory-identification problem (only proprietary position data — Vasquez et al. Cboe — resolves it). Treat the flag as probabilistic; cap its weight; A/B the multiplier off to measure contribution before trusting it live.
- **NIFTY transfer risk**: strongest microstructure results are US equities/E-mini/KOSPI; NSE corroboration exists for imbalance horizons (Tripathi–Dixit–Vipul; Gupta–Tripathi) and BankNifty futures (arXiv 2025, T3) but not for options-chain hedge flow at intraday scale. All kept trigger/regime features need a 4-week in-house shadow-logging validation before any advisory weight.
- **Expiry-calendar drift**: NSE moved index expiry Thu→Tue (Sep 2025); historical expiry-day stats (MarketNetra 2023–24) are Thursday-regime. Range/u-shape magnitudes should be re-estimated on Tuesday data as it accrues; keep the 1.25–1.30 multiplier provisional.
- **Regime-flag proliferation**: momentum-vs-reversal conditioning now has GEX-local, illiquidity, time-of-day, and intensity dimensions; interaction states can overfit on ~20 trading days/month. Enforce hierarchy (intensity/illiquidity > tod > GEX-local) and single-multiplier composition, not free-form LLM combination, until sample accrues.
- **Cost realism**: Jindal–Bajpai–Yadav show NSE OIB alpha dies after costs at hourly scale; scalping triggers must clear brokerage+STT+impact hurdle in simulation before the LLM ever sees them as triggers — keep the trigger/regime/context role separation enforced in code, not prompt.
