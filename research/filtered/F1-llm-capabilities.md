## Kept claims

- **LLMs must not do arithmetic on financial tables.** Even frontier models carry 4–8% numeric error (Claude-Sonnet-4 95.6%); error grows up to ~51% with computation depth and up to 47pp under numeric perturbation; small open models fail 50–70% of masked table values. [tier: A — FAITH, ICAIF'25; B — V-FiLLM, FININDICES; R02 S6,S12,S18]
- **Formatting of numbers changes model behavior.** Tokenization/decimal grouping swings arithmetic accuracy; errors are systematic. Consequence: one canonical rounding/format in the snapshot. [tier: B — Singh & McCoy; numeracy survey; R02 S9,S10]
- **Extraneous numbers in context measurably degrade output** (≥10%, worst −51.55% on open models). Snapshot minimalism is accuracy engineering, not aesthetics. [tier: B — R02 S11]
- **Code-computes / LLM-narrates is the validated hybrid.** FinRobot production pattern (pure-Python operators, numeric provenance); FinCon external CVaR; structured inputs + analyst-workflow CoT lifted GPT-4 52%→60% directional accuracy, above analysts' 53–57%. [tier: A — FinCon NeurIPS'24; B — FinRobot industry, Kim–Muhn–Nikolaev Booth working paper; R02 S8,S15,S17]
- **Parametric look-ahead bias makes in-training-window backtests meaningless.** Recall probes collapse to ~0 at the training cutoff; correcting memorization cuts in-sample returns up to 67.1%; standard LLMs show significant alpha decay vs point-in-time models. [tier: B, independently replicated ≥4× — R02 S2,S3,S4,S5; R01 S8,S9]
- **Published LLM trading-agent alpha is not deployment evidence.** FINSABER's 2-decade/100+ symbol/cost-inclusive re-test reversed FinMem's headline results (MSFT Sharpe 1.44→−1.247); 35/40 system×friction cells unmodeled across five named systems; net-of-friction reproductions fall below Buy&Hold; median backtest in the literature is 1.3 years. [tier: A — FINSABER KDD; B — Alpha Illusion, survey (Columbia/NYU); R01 S6,S7,S17]
- **LLM timing/regime skill is absent or negative.** FinAgent Sharpe 0.12 bull / −0.38 bear; FinMem −0.19/−0.97; trivial ATR-Band beats both in every regime; production fleets sized positions volatility-blind (median 5× leverage in all vol sextiles). [tier: A/B — R01 S6,S14] → regime detection and exposure caps must be deterministic.
- **LLMs over-extrapolate momentum from raw price series**, opposite to short-horizon reversal; resistant to prompt engineering. Do not feed raw OHLC and ask direction — exactly wrong for intraday scalping. [tier: B — Gulen et al.; R02 S7]
- **LLMs are useful as classifiers/interpreters with structured prompts**: post-cutoff headline classification predicts short-horizon direction (though tradable edge ≈34bps/day pre-cost); risk/uncertainty elicitation is better calibrated than point forecasts (80% intervals better calibrated than humans'). [tier: A — Lopez-Lira & Tang JFE-forthcoming; B — Gulen; R02 S1,S7]
- **Multi-agent debate doesn't pay for us.** Majority voting captures most debate gains (7 NLP benchmarks); homogeneous debate costs 2.1–3.4× tokens for equal/lower accuracy with sycophancy failure modes. [tier: A — NeurIPS'25; B — arXiv 2605.00914; R01 S18,S19]
- **Raw option chains exceed LLM reliability/context** — direct text-to-code over chains hallucinates invalid tickers/constraints; recommended pattern is pre-filtering + constrained DSL/structured output + deterministic validation. [tier: B — OQL; R01 S21]
- **Sub-15-minute LLM prediction is unevaluated-to-negative territory.** QuantAgent (best "fast" framework) works at 1–4h bars and admits failure on 1–15min candles; benchmarks lack minute-level/options support. Our value-add must come from computed microstructure + human execution, not LLM speed-reading of ticks. [tier: B — QuantAgent, QFinZero (ACL demo); R01 S16,S22]
- **Exits are where agents bleed**: production agents let 43.2% of positions reach ≥+300bps favorable excursion yet closed ~half of those negative; a mechanical bracket recovered +39bps/position. Bracket SL/target from ATR must be computed deterministically at advice time. [tier: B, large-n live record — R01 S14]

- **InvestLM expert-rated comparability**: no P&L/analysis outcome endpoint; dropped as decision-irrelevant. [R01 S20]
- **WSJ aggregate-market predictability (OOS R² ≈1.17%)**: statistically real, economically negligible at our horizon/cost; dropped as inapplicable to intraday index options. [R02 S13]

## Build directives

1. **Engine MUST pre-compute every number the LLM sees or quotes** — Greeks, OI change %, PCR, max pain, IV rank, ATR, VWAP deviation, candle stats, position P&L. The LLM input JSON contains no arithmetic to perform, and the LLM is forbidden from computing, scaling, or aggregating numbers in its output. [R02 S6,S9,S12,S15,S18]
2. **Snapshot MUST be compact, canonical, and minimal**: only decision-relevant fields, uniform decimal precision everywhere, short textual labels pre-generated by the engine (e.g., "aggressive call writing at 25,000"). No raw OHLC series, no full option-chain dump; pre-filter chain deterministically (e.g., ATM±5 strikes, liquid expiries, OI threshold). [R02 S9,S11,S18; R01 S21]
3. **LLM output MUST conform to a fixed schema** — direction, instrument/strike/expiry (chosen only from the pre-filtered candidate list embedded in the snapshot), entry band, stop, target, rationale, risk-flags — and a deterministic validator MUST reject malformed, constraint-violating, or unsourced-number output before the human sees it. Every numeric token in the rationale is string-matched against engine-computed fields (numeric provenance). [R01 S5,S21; R02 S15]
4. **Risk limits, regime gating, and bracket exits MUST be deterministic and external to the LLM**: engine computes vol regime, exposure caps, and ATR-based SL/target attached to every advisory; LLM may veto/flag but never sizes or widens stops. LLM confidence is displayed as a label, never as a probability, pending empirical calibration. [R01 S6,S7,S14; R02 S17]
5. **Topology MUST be single-agent, single-call with a fixed analyst-workflow CoT scaffold** ("state regime → state OI/vol evidence → bull case → bear case → invalidation"), optionally adding one embedded adversarial ("what kills this trade?") section. Do NOT build same-model multi-agent debate. [R01 S18,S19; R02 S8]
6. **Advice tasking MUST be classification/interpretation, not forecasting**: pattern-continuation vs exhaustion, key levels under threat, event-risk flags, invalidation conditions. Never "predict the next candle." [R02 S1,S7,S8; R01 S6]
7. **Latency/cost posture**: poll at 1–5 min cadence; LLM advice is decision-support for a human executor, never execution-grade. Any feature requiring sub-minute reaction must be deterministic. [R01 S16,S23; R02 §open-q5]
8. **Model selection**: use a frontier reasoning-class model for advice quality but treat this as *not* removing numeric fragility; re-run directive 3's provenance checks on every model swap. Evaluate finance-tuned smaller models only against the same harness. [R02 S6,S14]

## Evaluation protocol for OUR system

1. **Shadow log from day one.** Every advisory is stored with the exact snapshot JSON, model version, prompt hash, timestamp, schema-validated output, and the engine-computed bracket. Since a human executes, log both *advice-as-given* and *execution-as-taken*. [R01 S6–S9 implications]
2. **Forward-only evaluation.** Never score the LLM on data inside its training window; treat any such backtest number as inflated up to ~2/3 and label it non-evidence. Grade only on realized forward outcomes at fixed horizons (5/15/30 min) using engine-recorded prices. [R02 S3,S4,S5]
3. **Score components separately**: (a) direction hit rate vs a coin-flip and vs a trivial deterministic baseline (e.g., VWAP-side momentum rule) — an LLM layer that can't beat the trivial baseline is ceremony; (b) invalidation quality — how often the stated stop/invalidation preceded the adverse move; (c) bracket capture — realized MFE/MAE vs the engine's ATR bracket; (d) calibration — bucket stated confidence vs realized hit rate before ever trusting it. [R01 S6,S7,S14; R02 S7]
4. **Net-of-cost accounting always**: deduct brokerage, STT, exchange charges, stamp duty, and slippage assumptions from every hypothetical advisory P&L. Gross Sharpe is reported only as an upper bound, FINSABER-style disclaimer attached. [R01 S6,S7,S15]
5. **Snapshot self-test (masked-value audit) quarterly**: run a FAITH-style probe on our own schema — mask/corrupt numeric fields and measure how often the model quotes a number not in the snapshot. Hallucination rate on *our* snapshot is a release-gate metric for model swaps. [R02 S6,S12]
6. **Human-in-the-loop metrics**: advice-acceptance rate, win-rate delta of accepted-vs-declined advice, and expectancy delta vs the human's unaided trades. The product claim is "improves a human scalper," so that is the primary endpoint. [R01 open questions; R02 S1 thin-edge caveat]
7. **Regime-stratified reporting**: all metrics broken out by engine-tagged regime (trend/range, high/low India-VIX) to detect the documented conservative-in-bull/aggressive-in-bear pathology early. [R01 S6]
